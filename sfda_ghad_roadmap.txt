# Title: Saudi SFDA GHAD Platform Roadmap (الهيئة العامة للغذاء والدواء - منصة غد)

## Branch 1: GHAD Account Setup & Management (إدارة حسابات منصة غد)
*   **Description:** The centralized electronic gateway for all SFDA services covering food, drugs, and medical devices.
*   **Node 1.1: New User Registration (إنشاء مستخدم جديد)**
    *   Platform: GHAD Portal (https://ghad.sfda.gov.sa)
    *   Action: Click "Register", enter email and password, agree to terms, and submit. Open the email verification link to activate the profile.
    *   Profile Completion: Log in to choose profile type (Saudi/Resident or GCC), input ID/Iqama number with the supporting ID attachment, enter full name (Arabic & English), mobile number, and complete setup.
*   **Node 1.2: Commercial Account Creation (إنشاء حساب تجاري جديد)**
    *   Action: Log in -> "Create Account" -> Select "Commercial Account".
    *   Requirements: Fill in basic business info, Investment Type (Local/Foreign/Mixed), CR Type (Establishment/Company), CR Number, Expiry Date, CR Attachment, address details, and attach the official Delegation Letter (خطاب تفويض).
    *   Output: SFDA reviews the application and issues the commercial account.
*   **Node 1.3: Access Existing Account (طلب الوصول لحساب تجاري موجود)**
    *   Action: Log in -> "Request Access On Existing Account".
    *   Action: Search by the SFDA-issued Account Number (e.g., C-0023), enter the establishment name, mobile, email, and attach the Delegation Letter to link the user to the company's active licenses.

## Branch 2: Medical Devices Licensing & Retail (ترخيص المنشآت والمستلزمات الطبية)
*   **Description:** Operational guide for establishing a medical device retail/wholesale entity and obtaining MDEL & MDMA.
*   **Node 2.1: Prerequisites & Documentation (المستندات الأساسية)**
    *   CR Requirement: Active Commercial Registration explicitly including wholesale/retail trade in medical devices and supplies.
    *   Municipal & Facility: Municipal license (Balady) covering the shop/site with an authenticated Ejar contract.
    *   Technical Requirements: Certified Quality Management System (ISO 13485 QMS), appointment of a Qualified Person (شخص مسؤول) as the liaison with SFDA, and documented Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for storage, distribution, and traceability.
*   **Node 2.2: Establishment License Issuance (ترخيص المنشأة - MDEL)**
    *   Platform: GHAD System
    *   Action: Submit the MDEL request, upload all technical and municipal files, pay fees upon approval, and receive the license.
    *   Annual Renewal Fees: Class A (7,500 SAR), Class B (5,000 SAR), Class C (2,500 SAR). (Timeline: 1 to 3 days for account, several weeks for license review).
*   **Node 2.3: Marketing Authorization (إذن التسويق - MDMA)**
    *   Action: Mandatory clearance for each specific medical device before market distribution.
    *   Fees: 1,000 SAR per device (Valid for 3 years, with an annual renewal fee of 500 SAR).
*   **Node 2.4: Official SFDA Support Channels**
    *   Unified Call Center: 19999
    *   Direct Portal: https://ghad.sfda.gov.sa
    *   Customer Journey Guide: https://customerjourney.sfda.gov.sa
