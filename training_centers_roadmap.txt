# Title: Saudi Training Centers Business Roadmap

## Node 1: Scoping & Preliminary Legal Setup
*   **Sub-node 1.1: Identify the Regulatory Authority**
    *   Action: Determine the supervisor based on the training type:
        - Admin, IT, & Vocational: TVTC (Technical and Vocational Training Corporation).
        - Health & Medical: SCFHS (Saudi Commission for Health Specialties).
        - E-Learning/Online: NELC (National eLearning Center).
    *   Note: The rest of this roadmap focuses on TVTC, which is the most common for standard training centers.
*   **Sub-node 1.2: Initial Approval & Commercial Registration (CR)**
    *   Platform: TVTC Private Training Portal & Saudi Business Center (SBC)
    *   Action: Obtain "Preliminary Approval" from TVTC to reserve the name, then issue the restricted CR for a "Training Center".

## Node 2: Facility Preparation & Municipal Licensing
*   **Sub-node 2.1: Physical Location Compliance**
    *   Action: Rent and prepare a facility meeting TVTC strict requirements.
    *   Requirements: Must be an independent building or dedicated floor, private entrance, equipped with surveillance cameras, and accessible for people with disabilities.
*   **Sub-node 2.2: Municipal License**
    *   Platform: Balady Platform
    *   Action: Issue the commercial activity license (requires the TVTC Preliminary Approval).
*   **Sub-node 2.3: Civil Defense Certificate**
    *   Platform: Salama Portal
    *   Action: Issue the safety certificate after installing required fire alarms and exits.

## Node 3: Final Training License Issuance
*   **Sub-node 3.1: Final License Request**
    *   Platform: TVTC Private Training Portal
    *   Action: Upload Balady license, Salama certificate, and facility photos. Request a physical inspection by TVTC.
*   **Sub-node 3.2: License Fees & Validity**
    *   Issuance Fee: 2,000 SAR (Valid for 5 years).
    *   Renewal Fee: 5,000 SAR.

## Node 4: Administrative & Academic Cadre
*   **Sub-node 4.1: Appoint Center Manager**
    *   Platform: Qiwa & TVTC Portal
    *   Manager Requirements: Saudi National, Minimum 25 years old, Fully dedicated (Not a government employee or registered in another private job), and usually holds a Bachelor's degree. Must be officially approved by TVTC.
*   **Sub-node 4.2: Trainer Accreditation**
    *   Platform: TVTC / Munarh (منارة) Platform
    *   Trainer Requirements: Must pass TVTC evaluation and testing to become a "Certified Trainer".
    *   Operational Rule: A trainer is legally restricted to a maximum of 6 training hours per day.

## Node 5: Course Accreditation & Operations
*   **Sub-node 5.1: Regular Course Accreditation (On-site)**
    *   Platform: Munarh (منارة) Platform
    *   Action: All training packages and courses must be uploaded and approved before advertising or selling tickets.
*   **Sub-node 5.2: Off-site Course Permit**
    *   Action: If a course is held in a hotel or external venue, a specific permit must be requested.
    *   Fee: 1,000 SAR per off-site course.
