# Title: Saudi Compliance & Renewal Alerts (جدول التنبيهات والمتابعة الدورية)

## Branch 1: Commercial Registration (تجديد السجل التجاري والرخص)
*   **Description:** Tracking the annual renewal of the Commercial Registration (CR) to maintain legal compliance.
*   **Node 1.1: Document Retrieval**
    *   Action: Search the client database (e.g., Google Drive) using the company name or number.
    *   Action: Locate and open the official Commercial Registration Certificate (شهادة السجل التجاري).
*   **Node 1.2: Date Calculation & Alerts**
    *   Action: Extract the "Issue Date" (تاريخ الإصدار) from the document. The Expiry Date is exactly one year later.
    *   Alert Rule: Log the data into the Alerts Table and set the "Alert Date" (تاريخ التنبيه) at least 1 month before the expiry date.

## Branch 2: Iqama & Visas (الإقامات وتأشيرات الخروج والعودة)
*   **Description:** Monitoring expatriate legal documents to prevent fines and travel bans.
*   **Node 2.1: Iqama Renewal (تجديد الإقامة)**
    *   Rule: Iqamas can be renewed for durations of 3, 6, 9, or 12 months.
    *   Alert Rule: Check the Iqama expiry date and set the alert exactly 1 month before expiration.
*   **Node 2.2: Exit/Re-entry Visas (تأشيرات الخروج والعودة)**
    *   Warning: If the visa expires while the employee is outside Saudi Arabia, they face strict return bans and the company faces fines.
    *   Alert Rule: Set the alert strictly 10 to 15 days before the visa expires to take immediate action (extend or ensure return).

## Branch 3: Platform Subscriptions (اشتراكات المنصات - قوى ومقيم)
*   **Description:** Tracking the annual subscription cycles for essential government platforms like Qiwa and Muqeem.
*   **Node 3.1: Data Verification**
    *   Action: Subscription dates cannot always be found in standard documents. You must log in directly to the client's account on the specific portal (Qiwa/Muqeem).
    *   Action: Find the initial subscription activation date. Log this into the Alerts Table and set the renewal alert for one year from that exact date.
